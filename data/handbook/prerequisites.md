# Prerequisites

Some courses require you to have completed another course first. The required
course and the minimum grade are recorded for each one.

A prerequisite counts only if the earlier course is marked completed and the
grade is equal to or above the minimum. A course you are currently enrolled in
does not count, because it has no grade yet.

If a grade is below the minimum, the prerequisite is not met. The student may
retake the earlier course, or ask the department for a waiver.
